# VMS300 farmer guide — updated edition

Temporary comic stock narrator,20% faster. Full controller visible during physical-control explanations. The reviewed procedure, safety instructions and light farm humour are retained.

Duration: about3:45. Sources: DeLaval VMS V300 Instruction Book §6.2 pp248–256; Manual (Closed Stall) §4.5.3 pp86–89; empty-stall test prerequisite §5.5.1 p240.

## Step 01

Righto, let's get this robot lined up and earning its keep. We'll recalibrate your VMS V300: cup pickup, camera cleaning and tube holding. We'll keep the fancy talk out in the pasture. It ain't helping milk the cows.

## Step 02

Only do this if you've been shown how to do it safely. Stay outside the arm area unless the switch is in Safe. Keep out of the stall and gate movement area until remote control is blocked. In Run, the arm can move at any time. Lock does not stop it.

## Step 03

At the control box, set the mode switch to Run if needed. Check the yellow light is on. Keep clear of the arm.

## Step 04

On the InControl screen, select the correct station. Choose Manual, Closed Stall, from the robot mode menu. Let the cow finish and leave. Wait for an empty stall and Manual on screen. No queue-jumping, even for the boss cow.

## Step 05

Press Lock on the control box. Check the white Lock light comes on, and this robot shows the lock symbol. This prevents another screen from taking control. One boss at a time. We've enough of those on a farm.

## Step 06

Keep clear: the arm moves immediately when calibration starts. On this panel, press the top-left and bottom-left buttons of the left-hand group together. That's Entrance Gate and Teat Cleaning, beside the gearwheel symbol. The arm moves to teat cup one.

## Step 07

These buttons now have different jobs. Follow the four teat-button arrows to move the arm across. Exit Gate moves it up. Feed Portion moves it down. Teat Cleaning is Confirm. Entrance Gate is Cancel. Same buttons, new chores. Sounds like farm life.

## Step 08

Check how the gripper lines up with the cup handle. Tap Confirm once. You'll hear the gripper click. If the position is right, press and hold Confirm. The arm moves to the next position. Measure twice, milk once. That's the spirit.

## Step 09

Need an adjustment? Tap an arrow for a small move across. Holding the button keeps the arm moving. Use Up or Down for height. Tap Confirm and check the gripper again. When it's right, press and hold Confirm. Small moves. Save the big swings for the golf course.

## Step 10

Repeat for the remaining teat cups and the teat cleaning cup. Cancel keeps the existing position. Same dance, different partner. This one's got a cup handle.

## Step 11

After the teat cleaning cup, the arm moves to camera cleaning. The glass must press gently against the sponge. Adjust with the same arrows, then press and hold Confirm. Too much pressure can damage the glass. Too little won't clean properly. Even a robot likes a clean pair of specs.

## Step 12

Check the arm's tube supports, called horns, against the tubes in the rack. Adjust with the same arrows, then press and hold Confirm. Repeat for the second tube-holding position. The arm returns to its usual position. The screen shows Manual. Two positions. No bonus points for skipping the second.

## Step 13

Time to check our homework. The cows are tough graders. Keep the stall empty and everyone clear. On the InControl screen, open Maintenance, then Test. Tap Milk Cups to test the milk cups. Also test Teat Cleaning Cup. Buttons one to four test individual milk cups. Watch each pickup and release.

## Step 14

Tap Camera Cleaning. Watch it clean against the sponge, rinse and return. Tap Hold Tubes. Watch the arm move into position and return. Check both work correctly. If the arm shakes or moves jerkily, contact DeLaval service.

## Step 15

When the checks are complete, press Lock again. Check the white light goes off. Choose the intended operating mode. The control lock stays on until you release it. Back to the cows. They'll be wondering why their hired hand took a coffee break.
